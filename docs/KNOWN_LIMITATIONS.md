# Known limitations

- This release represents one prescribed, idealized supercell. The composites omit pressure and density evolution, thermodynamics, microphysics, friction and turbulent closure. The diagnostics express instantaneous procedural kinematics.
- Cloud, precipitation, terrain, condensation and debris are visual models. Cloud textures use a prescribed height-dependent advection profile, with envelopes aligned to the procedural storm. Condensation and debris use an integrated visual spin phase. Illustrative funnel shapes leave the single physical circulation intact. The case has no simulated subvortices, central downdraft, damage or entrainment.
- Stage times are instructional markers. The start of the near-surface vortex, onset of condensation and debris appearance occur on independent prescribed schedules.
- Instantaneous flow markers trace frozen-time streamlines. Forward parcel paths integrate evolving wind from new seeds at the selected time and are separate from those markers. Paths are short and regenerate when the scene updates. Ground-relative wind adds a constant translation while the camera domain stays storm-centered.
- Natural overlay compositing retains the nearest diagnostic fragment per pixel. X-ray is available for comparing transparent or intersecting layers. Light transport uses short sampled sun paths and empirical sky and multiple-scattering terms.
- Quantitative sections are horizontal. Both sections and signed 3D thresholds sample bounded lattices. Section color limits are fixed across time at the chosen extent. Storm-scale grids deliberately undersample the tornado core. A 5 m derivative stencil does not imply that a 46 km raster or isosurface resolves 5 m features.
- WebGL 2 is required. The cloud atlas limits resolvable cloud detail, and view-ray sampling can leave fine grain at Low quality. There is no temporal accumulation. Seeking, camera moves and moving diagnostics would require separate history invalidation before accumulation could be added. Particle positioning and isosurface extraction run on the CPU, where diagnostic refresh can briefly interrupt rendering. The fullscreen control depends on the browser's Fullscreen API.
