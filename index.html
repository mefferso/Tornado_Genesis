Science & methods

This is a conceptual right-moving supercell with procedural, quantitative wind fields. The lifecycle prescribes the appearance and evolution of kinematic features. It does not solve the governing equations.

Vorticity, convergence, tilting and stretching are finite-difference diagnostics of one wind field. Cloud, rain, condensation, outflow geometry and surface debris are qualitative. The circulation guide outlines the prescribed radius of peak azimuthal speed in the tornado component.

Flow markers travel along streamlines calculated on a frozen-time velocity field. Particle spacing and animation speed do not represent parcel observations or time-dependent trajectories.

The lifecycle is one training example. Different storms can organize near-ground rotation through different pathways.
